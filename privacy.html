Privacy Policy

Metabyl Group LLC · last updated 12 August 2026

This policy describes how Metabyl Group LLC ("Metabyl", "we") handles personal information in connection with its software products and its internal operational notification service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Information we collect

  • Mobile telephone number. Collected only where the account holder has entered their own number directly into a Metabyl system's configuration in order to receive notifications about that system.
  • Operational data. Records of scheduled obligations, application crash reports and tester feedback drawn from the operator's own developer accounts.
  • Message delivery records. Timestamps and delivery status retained by our messaging provider for troubleshooting.

How mobile numbers are used

A mobile number provided to Metabyl is used for one purpose only: sending operational notifications to the person who provided it. Numbers are not sold, rented, traded, or disclosed to any third party for marketing purposes, and are not used to build advertising audiences.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with activity and is typically one to two messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Metabyl does not charge for these messages; any charge comes from your own mobile carrier under your plan.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for assistance. Opt-out requests are honoured automatically and immediately by our messaging provider.

Service providers

We use Twilio Inc. to deliver SMS messages and Supabase Inc. to host application data. These providers process data solely to perform the service and are bound by their own agreements not to use it for their own purposes.

Retention

Notification records are retained only as long as needed to operate the service and are deleted when an account or system is decommissioned.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the address below.

Changes
