Programme: Metabyl Notifications — automated operational alerts sent by Metabyl Group LLC to the account holder who configured them. Message frequency: approximately 1–2 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

1. What this programme sends

Metabyl Notifications delivers automated operational messages to the person who set up the service. Messages cover two things: reminders about recurring administrative obligations that the account holder has entered into the system, and alerts about newly reported application crashes or tester feedback drawn from the account holder's own App Store Connect and Google Play Console accounts. This programme sends no marketing or promotional content of any kind.

2. How you join

There is no public sign-up. A person receives these messages only if they have entered their own mobile number into the configuration of a Metabyl system they operate. You may also text START to the programme number to resume messages after opting out.

3. How you leave

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages after that. Opt-outs are processed automatically by our messaging provider and take effect immediately.

4. Help

Reply HELP to any message for information about the programme, or contact us at the address below.

5. Message frequency and charges

Frequency varies with activity and is typically one to two messages per day. Metabyl charges nothing for these messages. Message and data rates may apply under your own mobile plan — contact your carrier for details of your plan's charges.

7. Supported devices

Messages are delivered by SMS to standard mobile handsets on major US carriers. Delivery to some devices, virtual numbers, or prepaid services may not be supported.
